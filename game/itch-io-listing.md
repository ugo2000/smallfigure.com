## 发布步骤（约 3 分钟）

1. 打开 https://itch.io/game/new ，登录账号。
2. **Title**：`Smallfigure: Merge Museum`
3. **Project URL**：建议 `smallfigure-merge-museum`（自动变 `you.itch.io/smallfigure-merge-museum`）
4. **Classification**：选 `Games`
5. **Kind of project**：选 `HTML`（关键，否则上传 zip 不会给"浏览器内玩"选项）
6. **Uploads** → `Upload files` → 选 `outputs/smallfigure-merge-museum-web.zip`
   - 传完后，在该文件行勾选 **"This file will be played in the browser"**
   - Embed 尺寸填 `420 × 868`
   - 勾 **"Mobile-friendly"**
7. **Cover image**：传 `outputs/cover-630x500.png`
8. **Screenshots**（可选但建议）：传 `game-menu.png`、`game-play.png`、`game-collection.png`
9. **Description**：复制下面「长描述」整段粘贴
10. **Tags**：`merge` `puzzle` `casual` `html5` `browser` `singleplayer` `collection` `cute` `portrait` `no-install` `family`
11. **Pricing**：
    - 想纯免费 → 选 `Free`
    - 想收打赏 → 选 `Name your own price`，最低填 `0`，itch.io 默认抽 10%（可在设置改）
12. **Privacy / 外部链接**：在"More information"里填隐私政策 `https://smallfigure.com/game/privacy.html`
13. 拉到顶部点 **Save** / **Publish** → 即时上线，无审核。

## 表单文案（直接复制）

**Tagline**：Drop. Merge. Collect 11 original tiny folks.

**Short description**（<150 字符）：
A cozy merge puzzle built from 11 hand-drawn characters. Drop a tiny folk, merge two of a kind, and fill the encyclopedia before the pile tops out.

**Long description**：
Smallfigure: Merge Museum is a small, calm merge puzzle made from the original character art of smallfigure.com. Every piece has a story: a crystal bell that only rings for lost things, a flame imp keeping a candle as a pet, an alien gentleman dressed for a dinner party on a planet that no longer exists.

Drop a character from the top of the board. Two of the same kind merge into the next, stranger character. Chain merges for combo bonuses, unlock all 11 entries in the museum, and try not to let the pile cross the red line.

No installs, no accounts, no timers, no energy. Just one more try.

Playable in the browser — English by default, with a one-tap Chinese toggle. Under 150 KB, loads instantly, zero external assets.

**How to play**：Move / drag to aim. Tap or click to drop. On a keyboard, use ← → and SPACE. Two identical characters merge on contact.

**Credits**：Original character art © 2026 smallfigure.com. Built with plain JavaScript; audio synthesised in code. No third-party art or music.
