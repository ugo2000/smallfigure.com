What we collect

我们收集什么

The game runs entirely inside your browser. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on any server. Your progress and your language choice are saved only in your device’s local browser storage (localStorage) and never leave your device.

游戏完全运行在你的浏览器里。我们不收集、传输或把任何个人信息存到服务器。你的游戏进度和语言偏好只保存在你本机浏览器的本地存储（localStorage）中，永远不会离开你的设备。

No ads, no trackers

无广告、无追踪

The self-hosted version on smallfigure.com loads no third-party advertising SDKs, no analytics, and no trackers. There are no external network requests after the page loads.

smallfigure.com 上的自托管版本不加载任何第三方广告 SDK、分析工具或追踪器。页面加载完成后没有任何外部网络请求。

If you play a copy distributed through a third-party platform (for example Poki, CrazyGames, or YouTube Playables), that platform may load its own SDK and advertising. In that case, the platform’s own privacy policy applies.

如果你玩的是通过第三方平台（例如 Poki、CrazyGames 或 YouTube 游戏大本营）分发的版本，该平台可能会加载它自己的 SDK 与广告。这种情况下以该平台的隐私政策为准。
