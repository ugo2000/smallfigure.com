## 描述
Every step you take is remembered. Press FREEZE and that walk becomes an echo: it replays
your steps from the start of the room, then stands still where you stopped.

A door opens only while every plate has someone standing on it. You cannot stand on a plate
and walk through the door at the same time — but an echo can stay behind and hold it for you.

Echoes move when you move, one step for one step. Press WAIT to stand still while your past
selves keep walking. Sometimes the whole puzzle is just letting them catch up.

10 rooms, 11 original hand-drawn tiny folks, English and 中文, no installs, no accounts,
no timers, no ads, no network calls. Under 140 KB.

Also playable directly at https://smallfigure.com/echo/
